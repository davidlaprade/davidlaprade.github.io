## Newsletter

I occasionally email new posts from [laprade.blog](https://laprade.blog). This
newsletter contains the post itself and a link to the published version. I manage
it myself, as a punishment for my sins.

If you're unfortunate enough to have made it onto my mailing list,
first: I'm sincerely sorry. Second: you can remove yourself at any point by clicking the
unsubscribe button. No hard feelings if you do. I'd probably unsubscribe if I
were you.

If, against all reason, you know someone who wants to be added to my mailing
list, just reply to one of the emails and let me know.

Google requires me to publish a [privacy policy](/newsletter/privacy/) and
[terms page](/newsletter/terms/) that describes how this newsletter works in
order for me to send the emails. You can take a look if you want, but they are
mostly performative and everything that matters is in the previous 3 paragraphs.
