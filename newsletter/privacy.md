## Newsletter Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

I store your name and email address to send you occasional posts from
[this blog's newsletter](/newsletter/). I do not sell your information or use it
for advertising.

You can unsubscribe whenever you want to stop receiving new messages. Just use the
unsubscribe link in any newsletter email. If you need help, email me at
[{{ site.author.email }}](mailto:{{ site.author.email }}).

I use Google to deliver emails and process unsubscribe requests. I
keep the information needed to run the newsletter and honor your choice to stop
receiving it. You can contact me to ask about your information or request a
correction or deletion.

I'll also happily send you the personal information I have
about you (name and email address) in the event that you forget them.

See also: [newsletter terms](/newsletter/terms/).
